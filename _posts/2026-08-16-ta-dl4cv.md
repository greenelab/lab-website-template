This position supports our teaching material on deep learning for computer vision and robotics, part of the lab's open-source education effort. You help prepare and maintain the hands-on part of the material: the exercises where students train, evaluate and deploy models on real perception data.

**What you will do**

- Develop and test lab exercises on 2D and 3D perception: detection, segmentation, depth and pose estimation.
- Prepare datasets and baseline models, and check that exercises run within a reasonable time and memory budget.
- Write solutions and grading notes, and improve the material based on student feedback.

**What we look for**

- A student or recent graduate with a solid background in deep learning and computer vision.
- Good Python and PyTorch skills; experience with ROS or robot data is a plus.
- Experience as a teaching assistant is welcome but not required.

The position is part-time and runs for a few months, with the exact hours and start date agreed together.

To apply, email [Reza Sabzevari](mailto:R.Sabzevari@tudelft.nl) with your CV, your transcript of records and a short motivation. TU Delft students should also include the information listed under *How to apply* on the [Vacancy](/vacancy) page.
