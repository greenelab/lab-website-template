Classical SLAM builds a geometric map: points, surfaces and a trajectory. Foundation models add semantics and language, so that a map can also answer questions such as *where is the fire extinguisher* or *which door leads to the lab*. This project studies how to combine the two without giving up the metric accuracy and real-time behaviour that robots need.

**What you will work on**

- Grounding open-vocabulary features from vision-language models into a metric SLAM map.
- Keeping the combined representation consistent over time, including when the robot revisits a place or the scene changes.
- Evaluating both the geometry, for example trajectory and reconstruction error, and the language grounding on real sequences.

**What we look for**

- An MSc student with background in SLAM or 3D vision, and in deep learning.
- Solid Python and PyTorch; experience with ROS and with running methods on real data is a plus.
