p4MARS develops open-source, interactive teaching material on machine learning and foundation models, so that students can experiment with modern perception and language models rather than only read about them. As a research assistant you help turn our research and course content into material that others can run.

**What you will do**

- Build interactive notebooks and small demos around vision-language and foundation models.
- Package models and datasets so they run reliably on student laptops and on TU Delft infrastructure.
- Write concise explanations and exercises to accompany each notebook, and test them with students.
- Maintain the public repository: issues, documentation and reproducible environments.

**What we look for**

- A BSc or MSc student, or a recent graduate, in computer science, robotics, aerospace or a related field.
- Solid Python and PyTorch; experience with transformers or vision-language models is a plus.
- Care for clear code and clear writing, since the result is meant to be read and reused by others.

The position is part-time and runs for a few months, with the exact hours and start date agreed together.

To apply, email [Reza Sabzevari](mailto:R.Sabzevari@tudelft.nl) with your CV, your transcript of records and a short motivation. TU Delft students should also include the information listed under *How to apply* on the [Vacancy](/vacancy) page.
