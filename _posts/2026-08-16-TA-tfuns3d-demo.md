This position supported the live demo built on **T-FunS3D**, our task-driven, open-vocabulary 3D functionality segmentation method, which was shown at Robots in Business 2026 at RoboHouse. The work consisted of turning a research prototype into a robot demo that runs reliably in front of an audience.

**What the position involved**

- Integrating T-FunS3D with navigation and manipulation on a mobile manipulator.
- Building the pipeline from a spoken or typed task to a target object, functional part and robot action.
- Making the system robust to a changing, crowded environment, and preparing a fallback mode for the event.
